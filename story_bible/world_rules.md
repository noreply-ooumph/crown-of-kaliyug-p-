# World Rules: Crown of Kaliyug

## 1. Divine Manifestation
- Gods do not walk the earth in glowing light. They appear as people.
- Krishna is an avatar, but until Season 5, he must be written as a man with extraordinary charisma and insight.
- [ABSOLUTE] No magic effects (energy beams, flying, etc.) before Season 4.

## 2. Dharma and Karma
- Dharma is not 'goodness.' It is 'duty.'
- Following one's duty often leads to tragedy (e.g., Bhishma fighting for the wrong side).
- Every action has a consequence that must be tracked in the `continuity_log`.

## 3. Visual Aesthetics
- **Hastinapur:** Stone, iron, elephants, grandeur.
- **Indraprastha:** Glass, water, reflections, impossible architecture.
- **Dwarka:** White stone on blue sea, cosmopolitan.

## 4. Continuity Constraints
- Fact revelation must follow the `protected_facts.yaml` timeline.
- Character death is permanent. No resurrections.

## 5. Character-Specific Rules
- **Draupadi:** Never passive. Even in the Assembly Hall, her presence must be active, questioning, and fierce.
- **Krishna:** Never lies. His truth is always there, but often hidden in plain sight.
- **Karna:** Always right about moral stakes, even when choosing the 'wrong' side.
