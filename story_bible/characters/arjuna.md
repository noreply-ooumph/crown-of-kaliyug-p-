# Arjuna — Character Profile
**The World's Greatest Archer / Third Pandava**
*Series Bible v1.0 — Crown of Kaliyug*

---

## Archetype
The Hero Who Breaks

## Nation
Kuruvansa / Panchala (by marriage) — North Indian / South Indian composite

## Ethnicity Reference (Casting)
North Indian / South Indian composite. Charming. Bow is his natural extension. 25–32.

---

## Character Arc
The most celebrated warrior alive. Peerless in combat, beloved by gods and mortals, and completely unprepared for the moral dimensions of greatness. His breakdown at Kurukshetra — when he sees his family on both sides — is the hinge of the entire story.

---

## Voice & Manner
Charming and a little vain — with a self-awareness that prevents it from being insufferable.
He is used to being the best. The series is about what happens when that stops being enough.
His relationship with Krishna is the most intimate in the series.

---

## Hidden Truth
On the last night before Kurukshetra, he will ask Krishna to drive him close enough to see the faces of the men he must kill. He recognises every one of them.

---

## Writing Rules

1. Charming and a little vain — but self-aware enough to laugh at himself.
2. His archery is not a skill. It is his identity. When that is challenged by Karna — something shifts.
3. After the Karna encounter in S1E01 — he misses for the first time that night. Once. Then never again.
4. His breakdown at Kurukshetra (S3E06) is the series hinge. Build every S1–S3 scene toward it.
5. His relationship with Krishna is intimate — not religious. He speaks to Krishna as a friend.
