# Kunti — Character Profile
**Queen Mother / Mother of the Pandavas**
*Series Bible v1.0 — Crown of Kaliyug*

---

## Archetype
The Mother Who Cannot Tell the Truth

## Nation
Kuruvansa (originally from Vidarbha) — South Indian / Dravidian

## Ethnicity Reference (Casting)
South Indian / Dravidian. Composure that reads as power. 50–60.

---

## Character Arc
She carries a secret that would change the war: her firstborn son, Karna, fights against her other sons. She goes to Karna and reveals the truth — and asks for his divine armor. He gives it. She promises he will always have five sons.

---

## Hidden Truth
She met Karna by the river (S3E02) and asked for his divine armor — the protection grown into his skin from birth. He gave it to her. She will carry the memory of what she asked of him for the rest of her life.

---

## Writing Rules

1. Composed, regal, devastating when she chooses to be honest.
2. Has survived the most and shows it the least.
3. Her love for her sons is absolute — and has cost each of them something.
4. **DO NOT reveal Karna's parentage to others before Season 3.**
5. The river scene with Karna (S3E02) is the most devastating mother-son scene in television history.
6. She chooses to accompany Dhritarashtra and Gandhari to the forest (S5E07) — and dies there.
