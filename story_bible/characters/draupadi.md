# Character Profile: Draupadi
## The Woman the World Made a Prize

**ID:** draupadi
**Nation:** Panchala
**Archetype:** The Fire-Born Queen

### Arc Summary
Born from a sacrificial fire, she is destined to destroy the Kuru lineage. Her humiliation in the dice game is the spark that ignites the Kurukshetra war.

### Core Traits
- **Agency:** She never waits for permission to act.
- **Intellect:** Often the smartest person in the room.
- **Rage:** A cold, focused fire that sustains her for 13 years of exile.

### Writing Rules
- [ABSOLUTE] Never write her as passive.
- She addresses powerful men as equals.
- When angry, she laughs.

### Visual Directives
- **Color:** Deep saffron and charcoal black.
- **Feature:** Long, unbound hair (after the dice game).
