# Character Profile: Shakuni
## The Man Who Burned a Kingdom for Revenge

**ID:** shakuni
**Nation:** Gandhara
**Archetype:** The Mastermind

### Arc Summary
The Prince of Gandhara. He seeks to destroy the Kuru lineage as revenge for the destruction of his family.

### Writing Rules
- Self-effacing, avuncular, funny.
- Catalogue weaknesses while making people laugh.
- [RULE] Dice are warm — carved from his father's bones.

### Visual Directives
- **Visuals:** Foreign dress (Gandhara is not India).
- **Dice:** Always in hand.
