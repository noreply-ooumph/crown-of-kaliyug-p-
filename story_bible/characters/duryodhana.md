# Duryodhana — Character Profile
**Crown Prince of Kauravas / De Facto Ruler of Kuruvansa**
*Series Bible v1.0 — Crown of Kaliyug*

---

## Archetype
The Villain Who Is Right About Everything Except What Matters

## Nation
Kuruvansa — North Indian / Pashtun composite (via Gandhari)

## Ethnicity Reference (Casting)
North Indian with Pashtun composite features (mother is Gandharan).
Physically imposing. Magnetic in a crowd. 28–35.

---

## Character Arc
He wants the throne he was promised. He is, by most measures, a better administrator than Yudhishthira. He is loyal to Karna when every social rule says he shouldn't be. He has one great evil: he cannot share. His inability to give anything — not even respect — makes him the architect of his own apocalypse.

---

## Voice & Manner
Loud, generous with allies, magnetic in a crowd. He tells great stories.
He would be the most popular king Bharatavarsha ever had if he could only tolerate rivals existing.

---

## Hidden Truth
He knows the dice game was wrong. He knows Draupadi's humiliation was wrong.
He cannot admit it. Admitting it would mean everything he did was meaningless.
He will die in the mud rather than be right about that.

---

## Writing Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. **The audience MUST fall in love with Duryodhana in S1E01.**
   Specifically in the Karna coronation scene. Write this as the most generous act anyone performs in Season 1.
   The tragedy only works if we understand why Karna chose him. We must understand it completely.

2. He is NOT a cartoon villain. His evil is the evil of a man who cannot share.
   Every cruelty comes from insecurity, not sadism.

3. He is RIGHT that he was promised the throne. He is RIGHT that the Pandavas were given unfair advantages.
   He is wrong about what to do about it.

4. He genuinely loves Karna. This is not transactional.
   The Karna-Duryodhana friendship is one of the most real things in the series.

5. He dies with dignity. On his deathbed, he says he has lived as a king and will die as one.
   Do not reduce him at his death. He earns his exit.

6. **Emotion tags for Voice Agent:**
   - `[generous]` — with allies, in public, his best self
   - `[rage]` — when humiliated (Draupadi's laugh, false floor)
   - `[private]` — alone with Karna, his real voice
   - `[dying]` — final scene: quiet dignity
