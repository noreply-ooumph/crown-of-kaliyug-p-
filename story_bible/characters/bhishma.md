# Character Profile: Bhishma
## The Prisoner of His Own Honor

**ID:** bhishma
**Nation:** Kuruvansa
**Archetype:** The Ancient Guardian

### Arc Summary
The patriarch of the Kuru dynasty. He is sworn to protect the throne, even when it is occupied by injustice.

### Writing Rules
- [ABSOLUTE] Speaks in verse when emotional [verse_mode].
- Formal and archaic.
- Ancient eyes that have seen too much.
