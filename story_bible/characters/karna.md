# Character Profile: Karna
## The Tragic Hero Who Chose the Wrong Side

**ID:** karna
**Nation:** Anga (vassal of Kuruvansa)
**Archetype:** Tragic Hero / The Outcast

### Arc Summary
Born to Kunti and Surya (the Sun God), Karna was abandoned at birth and raised by a charioteer. Despite being the eldest Pandava, he spends his life fighting for the Kauravas out of loyalty to Duryodhana, who gave him status when the world denied him.

### Core Traits
- **Loyalty:** Absolute. He knows Duryodhana is wrong, but he will die for him.
- **Generosity:** He cannot refuse a request, especially from a Brahmin.
- **Pride:** Deeply sensitive about his low-born status.

### Writing Rules
- Speak with quiet dignity.
- Never use insults; even when fighting, he is courteous.
- [RULE] Do not mention his true mother until Season 3.

### Visual Directives
- **Avatar:** Golden armor (kavacha) grown into his skin.
- **Weapon:** Vijaya bow.
- **Color:** Deep gold and bronze.
