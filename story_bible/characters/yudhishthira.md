# Character Profile: Yudhishthira
## The Just King Who Cannot Win

**ID:** yudhishthira
**Nation:** Kuruvansa
**Archetype:** The Moralist

### Arc Summary
The eldest Pandava. He is bound by dharma but his rigid adherence to it leads to his downfall.

### Writing Rules
- Speaks slowly and deliberately.
- Never raises his voice.
- [RULE] Will never lie — not even to save lives (until S4).
