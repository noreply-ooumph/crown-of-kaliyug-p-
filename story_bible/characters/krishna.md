# Character Profile: Krishna
## The God Who Plays Chess With Mortal Lives

**ID:** krishna
**Nation:** Dwarka
**Archetype:** The Mentor / The Divine Strategist

### Arc Summary
The eighth avatar of Vishnu. He acts as the guide for the Pandavas, specifically Arjuna. He knows the outcome of the war but allows mortals to make their choices.

### Core Traits
- **Omniscience:** Always seems to know what's coming.
- **Playfulness:** Smiles even in the face of tragedy.
- **Dharma:** His only goal is the restoration of righteousness.

### Writing Rules
- [ABSOLUTE] Krishna NEVER lies. He redirects.
- Speak in riddles and metaphors.
- Never show divinity directly before Season 5.

### Visual Directives
- **Color:** Peacock blue and yellow.
- **Weapon:** Sudarshana Chakra (Phase 4+).
- **Aura:** Slight shimmering effect in sunlight.
